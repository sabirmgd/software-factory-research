# Experiment result template

**Status: TEMPLATE — no run represented.** Replace this status only with a real observation record.

## Identity and hypothesis
Run ID, date, evaluator, hypothesis, task or population, source revision, and comparison condition.

## Setup and authority
Environment, models/tools, approved scope, safeguards, time/attempt budget, and known prior exposure to the task.

## Observations
Actual measured values with units and denominator; failures, skipped checks, waiting, missing data, and evidence pointers. Separate setup costs from recurring effort. Never substitute expected outcomes for observations.

## Interpretation and limits
What mechanism is supported or challenged? What alternative explanation remains? What was not tested? Include negative effects and confidence calibrated to the evidence.

## Decision and follow-up
Retain, change, reject, or inconclusive. Name the next bounded experiment or product decision, its owner, and any remediation required.

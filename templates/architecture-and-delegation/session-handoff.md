# Session handoff

**Reusable template.** Use when context will reset or another contributor needs to act. Keep facts durable without exporting the whole conversation.

[Template guide](../README.md) · [Filled or worked reference](../../examples/task-assignment/implementation-outline.md)


## Current objective and revision
Task ID, source commit, work branch, approved scope, and relevant contracts.

## What changed
Behavior and file delta. Distinguish committed, uncommitted, proposed, and discarded work. Identify concurrent work to avoid collisions.

## Evidence
Exact commands, environment, exit status, test scope, artifacts, and known gaps. Do not say "all tests pass" when only a subset ran.

## Decisions and authority
Accepted decisions with owner and date. Pending decisions, prohibited actions, and remaining attempt or time budget.

## Next action
One bounded next task, its expected result, relevant source pointers, and the first verification command. Include a stop condition if evidence contradicts the assumptions.

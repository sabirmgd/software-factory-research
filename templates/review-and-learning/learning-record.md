# Factory learning record

**Reusable template.** Use for a recurring or consequential problem, not as mandatory ceremony after every commit.

[Template guide](../README.md) · [Filled or worked reference](../../research/evolution-of-the-ideas.md)


## Event and evidence
Describe the error, delay, misunderstanding, or unexpected success. Link actual instances and distinguish one event from a recurring class.

## Diagnosis
Was the problem in product understanding, authority, architecture, implementation, evidence, explanation, environment, or delivery? Consider alternatives before adding a rule.

## Change at the right level
Prefer eliminating the source of an error when feasible. Otherwise consider types, deterministic checks, tests, a reference example, or guidance. Sometimes deleting a rule or simplifying the workflow is the improvement.

## Proof and cost
Show that a new check catches a relevant violation and accepts a valid case. Record false positives, runtime, maintenance owner, and what would justify removal.

## Scope
Is this reusable foundation, project-specific meaning, or an experiment? Protect client details. Name an evaluation date and do not call the factory improved until evidence supports it.

# Outcome evaluation

**Reusable template.** Use after enough real use to evaluate an outcome hypothesis. Shipping alone does not populate this record.

[Template guide](../README.md) · [Filled or worked reference](../../examples/customer-outcome/outcome-evaluation.md)


## Hypothesis and context
Link the original hypothesis, intervention, affected population, delivery dates, and exposure/adoption evidence. State what actually reached users.

## Observations
Baseline and post-change results with units, denominators, missing data, time window, and collection method. Include qualitative findings and harms, not just favorable numbers.

## Interpretation
What supports or weakens the mechanism? What confounds, selection effects, or concurrent changes prevent causal conclusions? Mark inconclusive results explicitly.

## Decision
Expand, revise, retain, stop, or collect more evidence. State accountable owner, support implications, and the next review point. A useful decision may be to remove software.

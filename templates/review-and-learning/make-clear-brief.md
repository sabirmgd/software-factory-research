# Make Clear brief

**Reusable template.** Use when understanding is costly or important. It may support exploration, teaching, or review; it does not automatically create an approval gate.

[Template guide](../README.md) · [Filled or worked reference](../../examples/task-assignment/make-clear-review.md)


## Understanding goal
What should the audience understand after viewing this? What do they already know? Is the task exploration, comparison, explanation, or a decision? State device and accessibility constraints.

## Source and status
List source revisions, code paths, requirements, observations, and tests. Label facts as observed, proposed, or not verified. Missing code access must not become an invented system diagram.

## Representation
Choose the smallest useful explanation, sequence, state model, comparison, before/after view, or disposable prototype. Use realistic labels and distinguish diagram edge meanings. Prefer a narrow mobile layout when relevant.

## Consequences and challenge
Show what changes, what does not, who is affected, failure paths, and unresolved assumptions. Include the strongest reason the proposal might be wrong. A beautiful picture is not evidence of correctness.

## Interaction and follow-through
Ask the audience to explain or challenge the important point. Record corrections in the owning source document, then regenerate the view. A decision is optional; when needed, record its scope and authority separately.

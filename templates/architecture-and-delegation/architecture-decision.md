# Architecture decision record

**Reusable template.** Use for a consequential choice with alternatives and ongoing consequences. Do not require an ADR for ordinary implementation details.

[Template guide](../README.md) · [Filled or worked reference](../../research/synthesis-decisions.md)


## Decision identity
ID, date, scope, status [proposed / accepted / superseded], decision owner, related records, and source revision.

## Context and forces
What problem requires a choice? What constraints, evidence, reversibility limits, and uncertainties matter? Distinguish foundational defaults from project-specific needs.

## Options and decision
Compare viable options, including retaining the existing approach where relevant. Explain the chosen mechanism and why alternatives were rejected. Approval remains pending until an authorized person accepts it.

## Consequences and enforcement
What becomes simpler? What becomes harder? What changes for callers, deployment, cost, privacy, or support? Identify executable checks and migration work.

## Revisit conditions
Specify observable conditions that would justify revisiting the choice. Include exception owner and expiration where applicable. Link superseding decisions rather than rewriting history invisibly.

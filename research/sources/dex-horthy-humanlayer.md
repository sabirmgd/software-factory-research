## RPI workflow

RPI's paths separate research, combined discussion, product requirements, technical design, outline, and implementation without requiring every artifact. The workflow allows direct implementation when the necessary decisions are already settled. Documents carry current decisions and unanswered questions across phases. [S01](index.md#s01)

## Product and technical design

The PRD process develops the user-facing problem and solution, including a success measure. Technical questions are deferred rather than ignored. The TDD process then discusses system interactions and program shape separately, obtaining user approval for both. Views such as call trees and diagrams support those discussions. [S02](index.md#s02) [S03](index.md#s03)

## What we adopt and what we change

We adopt inspectable decisions and durable handoffs. We make research autonomous only inside explicit access authority. We use compact artifacts for familiar work and preserve human review for consequential changes. Those are our operating choices, not additional guarantees of the skill.

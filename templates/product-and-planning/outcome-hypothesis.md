# Outcome hypothesis

**Reusable template.** Use when a meaningful change is justified by a user or customer benefit. A tiny maintenance task can state its purpose in its work item instead.

[Template guide](../README.md) · [Filled or worked reference](../../examples/customer-outcome/outcome-evaluation.md)


## People and problem
Who experiences the problem? What concrete task do they perform? Describe observed difficulty separately from a stakeholder's interpretation. Link evidence with date, population, and limits. Do not invent a baseline.

## Proposed intervention and alternatives
What is the smallest useful intervention? Compare doing nothing, improving a process or data source, and creating or changing software where relevant. What would make the requested feature unnecessary?

## Hypothesis
We believe [intervention] will help [people] perform [task] with [desired improvement], because [mechanism]. This is a prediction, not a result.

## Observation plan
Baseline: [known value / unavailable and collection plan]. Signal: [behavior or qualitative observation]. Unit and denominator: [definition]. Population and observation window: [definition]. Evaluation owner: [accountable person].

## What must not worsen
Name safety, privacy, reliability, accessibility, support, and workflow constraints that matter. State stop conditions and a recovery owner.

## Interpretation
What would support, weaken, or leave the hypothesis inconclusive? Note competing explanations and seasonal or population differences. Decide when to expand, revise, stop, or collect more evidence.

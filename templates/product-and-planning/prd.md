# Product requirements document

**Reusable template.** Use when user-facing behavior or success criteria need agreement. Skip a standalone PRD when an existing use case already settles them.

[Template guide](../README.md) · [Filled or worked reference](../../examples/task-assignment/prd.md)


## Problem to solve
Describe the user problem and relevant evidence. Link the outcome hypothesis and use cases instead of copying them wholesale.

## Success and guardrails
State what success would look like after use, how it will be evaluated, and what must not worsen. Separate product hypotheses from acceptance conditions.

## Proposed solution
Explain behavior in user language. Identify actors, important states, empty and error states, and UI expectations where relevant. Link a prototype only when it answers a meaningful question.

## Solution details
Give the important interaction sequences, alternatives, scope boundaries, and data visibility expectations. A permission matrix may describe intended behavior without prescribing its implementation.

## Acceptance criteria
Assign IDs and concrete examples, including failure and denial. Define what is intentionally outside scope.

## Decisions and unresolved items
Record accepted choices and who accepted them; otherwise label draft. Technical choices about modules, storage, and dependency structure belong in the technical design. List what is deferred, not silently assumed.

## Outcome follow-through
Evaluation owner, observation window, adoption or support dependencies, and conditions for revising the product.

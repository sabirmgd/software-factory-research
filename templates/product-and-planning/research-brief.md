# Bounded research brief

**Reusable template.** Use when a decision depends on facts not yet established. A quick lookup may be recorded inline instead.

[Template guide](../README.md) · [Filled or worked reference](../../examples/task-assignment/research.md)


## Decision being informed
What question must be answered? What choice will change depending on the findings? Stop when the next responsible step is adequately supported.

## Scope and budget
Repositories, commits, documentation, tests, interfaces, or customer evidence in scope. Explicit exclusions. Time or action budget. Permission to run commands, use external systems, or create scratch artifacts. Read-only is the default for production state, not a claim that every command is harmless.

## Findings
For each finding record source location and revision, observed fact, interpretation, and uncertainty. Separate current code behavior from intended behavior and historical documentation.

## Contradictions and gaps
What conflicts? Which paths could not be exercised? What has not been inspected? Do not turn missing results into proof of absence.

## Decision-ready handoff
Summarize the facts that matter, remaining questions and owners, and the next smallest useful action. Link durable artifacts. Do not silently approve architecture or expand scope.

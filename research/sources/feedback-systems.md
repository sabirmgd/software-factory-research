# Feedback systems and delivery evidence

DORA's small-batch guidance supports learning through short feedback cycles; its WIP guidance looks at the complete stream of unfinished work; its customer-feedback guidance connects product decisions to customer behavior. These are supporting sources for our model, not direct experimental validation of agent orchestration or Make Clear. [S11](index.md#s11) [S12](index.md#s12) [S13](index.md#s13)

Google's SRE chapter describes canarying as limited exposure evaluated before wider rollout. We use it as a release-containment example. The chapter does not imply that every side effect is reversible or that every project needs the same deployment strategy. [S23](index.md#s23)

Our three-loop model and proposed attention measurements are original synthesis. They should be tested on real work with setup cost, context, and alternative explanations recorded.
